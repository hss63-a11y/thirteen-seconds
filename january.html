2019Jan 2020 · at Houston

Wild Card · Texans

Buffalo led 16–0 early in the third quarter. Allen ran, caught a touchdown pass on a trick play and broke Jim Kelly's franchise record for postseason QB rushing. Houston came back anyway and won it in overtime. A preview of the next six years, delivered efficiently.

2020Jan 2021 · at Kansas City

AFC Championship · Chiefs

Buffalo's first AFC title game since the 1993 season. Allen won two playoff games to get there. In Kansas City he spent most of the night running from the pass rush, and the Chiefs pulled away in the second half.

2021Jan 2022 · at Kansas City

Divisional · Chiefs

The thirteen-seconds game. Allen threw for 329 yards and four touchdowns, all to Gabe Davis, and led Buffalo ahead with 13 seconds left. Kansas City needed two completions and a field goal to tie, then won the overtime coin toss and scored. See the play-by-play.

Patrick Mahomes vs. Josh Allen, postseason
Four meetings. Four–oh. One was in Buffalo.

Allen's defenders like to point out that he beats Mahomes in the regular season. That's true, and it's the problem. Nobody hands out rings in October.
