Firebaugh, Calif. → Laramie, Wyo. → Orchard Park, N.Y.

Origin story.

Nobody wanted him. Then everybody did. Here's how a cotton-farm kid with zero Division I offers became the most expensive thing in Western New York that can't beat the Chiefs.

The road, in order

Everyone who passed had a point

Every coach who said no turned out to be right for at least a little while.

  1. 2014Firebaugh, Calif.

    The farm kid nobody recruited

    Allen grew up on a 3,000-acre cotton farm about 40 miles west of Fresno and graduated from Firebaugh High School in 2014. He was a Fresno State fan who went to their camps. Fresno State's coaches watched him and passed. So did every other Division I program in the country. San Diego State offered him a chance to walk on, and he turned it down.

    Verdict  Fresno State saw something. We just don't know what.

  2. 2014Reedley College

    Junior college

    He went to Reedley College, partly because one of the assistant coaches was married to his cousin. He threw 26 touchdowns there, grew to 6'5", and started getting looks from bigger programs.

    Verdict  Family connections: the original free square.

  3. 2015Laramie, Wyo.

    Two offers. Then one.

    Only Wyoming and Eastern Michigan offered him a scholarship. Eastern Michigan pulled its offer when he visited Wyoming. So the future MVP went to Laramie, a town above 7,000 feet where the wind does most of the passing.

    Verdict  Eastern Michigan dodged a decade of heartbreak.

  4. 2017Final college season

    1,812 yards

    His last season at Wyoming: 1,812 passing yards, 16 touchdowns, 6 interceptions. Draft analysts argued about his accuracy for months. Then teams watched him throw at his pro day and forgot all of it.

    Verdict  The arm talks. The stat sheet mumbles.

  5. 2018Round 1 · Pick 7

    Buffalo trades up

    The Bills took him seventh overall. As a rookie he led all quarterbacks in rushing touchdowns and posted the league's lowest completion rate among qualified passers, 52.8%. Both of those facts would turn out to be predictive.

    Verdict  Every scout was right. Then he got better, which nobody asked for.

  6. 2025March · Buffalo

    $330 million

    Seven years after the draft, the kid with no Division I offers signed a six-year, $330 million extension with $250 million guaranteed. Wyoming put him in its athletics Hall of Fame the same year.

    Verdict  A great story. It still doesn't have an ending.

The moral
Overlooked by everyone. Overrated in January.

It is a great underdog story, and that's what makes it so frustrating. Every chapter has a payoff except the last one. Until it does, he's the best quarterback in the league never to reach a Super Bowl. That is a real title, and this site intends to keep it updated.
