The grades

Scouts grade tools from 20 to 80, where 50 is average and 80 is the best there is. He has several 80s. He also has a 20.

Flags on the play

The penalties

Every one of these happened. The rulings are our own.

Fumbled snap at the goal line

Week 10, 2022 · vs. Minnesota

Buffalo was at the Vikings' 1 with seconds left in regulation. Allen fumbled the snap, and Minnesota recovered it in the end zone for a touchdown. In overtime he threw the game-losing interception in the red zone. Vikings 33, Bills 30.

Ruling Loss of game, twice in one afternoon

Four-turnover season opener

Week 1, 2023 · at N.Y. Jets

Four turnovers and one touchdown on Monday Night Football. He still dragged Buffalo to overtime, where the Jets won on a punt return. Jets 22, Bills 16.

Ruling Delay of season

Illegal hurdle

2018 vs. Anthony Barr · 2024 vs. Budda Baker

A 6'5" quarterback should not jump over NFL linebackers. He did it as a rookie in Minnesota and was still doing it six years later against Arizona, hurting his non-throwing hand on the play.

Ruling Unsportsmanlike: makes everyone else's quarterback look boring

Intentional grounding of hope

2018–19 · league-wide

Last among qualified passers in completion rate two years running. The scouts who said he couldn't hit open receivers were right for two seasons, which is exactly long enough to be wrong.

Ruling Overturned on review, 2020

The “low positive” era

Midseason 2023

During a six-game slump full of turnovers, Allen said he'd been trying a "low positive" mentality and wondered whether he had reined himself in too much. The offensive coordinator was fired.

Ruling Illegal formation, mental

Excessive compensation

March 2025 · contract extension

Six years, $330 million, $250 million of it guaranteed. The money is for winning in January. So far it's covering September through mid-January.

Ruling Holding, of the team's cap space

2024 NFL Most Valuable Player
27 of 50 first-place votes.

He won it, and he deserves the credit. Some fine print, though. He got 54% of the first-place votes. He became only the third quarterback to win MVP without being named first-team All-Pro. And it was announced eleven days after his season ended in Kansas City, again.
